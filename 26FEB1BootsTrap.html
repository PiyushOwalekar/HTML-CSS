Precision Engineering

BMW M Power represents the perfect harmony between track-ready performance and daily drivability. Every component is tuned at the Nürburgring to ensure the "Ultimate Driving Machine" isn't just a slogan, but a promise. The M5 CS, for instance, features a 4.4-liter V8 engine with M TwinPower Turbo technology, delivering a staggering 627 horsepower.
